# /brag plan: GrokBot Local, kinetic motion cut

**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps with 3-sub-frame motion blur. **Length:** 20.5 s at 120 BPM (one beat is 0.5 s).
**Tone:** kinetic motion graphics in the style of an Apple or Linear launch. The type lands on the beat, the product UI moves through 3D space, the transitions are match cuts, and the pace is fast but every line holds long enough to read.
**Angle:** same as the first cut, "the agent is on your computer, and you stay in control", now told through motion.
**Visual identity:** the app's real stylesheet, class names, copy and orb logo, with Inter and Lucide icons. The only accent colors are the app's own green (#4ade80) and amber (#fbbf24).

## Sound
An original track at 120 BPM in A minor. The intro has no kick, then a riser leads into a drop at 3.0. From there it has a four-on-the-floor kick, sidechain pumping on the pad and bass, and an arpeggio from 9.5. It breaks down at 16.9 to the final impact and a chime. The sound effects are in key and share the music's reverb. The music ducks about 10 dB under the voice.
